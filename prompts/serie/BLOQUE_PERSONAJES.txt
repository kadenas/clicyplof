Two small figures made of the same bright medium blue modelling clay (not navy, not dark blue), matte, with visible fingerprints. Both have a big round ball-shaped head, two small black dot eyes and a thin curved black smile; no nose, no ears, no hair, no clothes. They look exactly like the two figures in the first frame and keep that exact look, size and colour until the last frame. They have very different silhouettes:
Character A is the tall thin one: a big round head, wider than its body, about one third of its height, on top of a long narrow sausage-shaped body, with long thin arms and long straight thin legs. A is NOT a stick figure and its head is NOT small.
Character B is the short round one, about two thirds of A's height: two balls of similar size stacked on top of each other, a big round head on top of a fat round belly, with short chubby arms and very short thick legs with small feet. B is NOT a shapeless lump.
